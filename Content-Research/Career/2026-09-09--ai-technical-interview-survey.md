## Summary

Karat surveyed 400 engineering leaders across the US, India, and China on
how their organizations actually handle AI in technical interviews. The
gap between regions and between stated policy and stated belief is the
interesting part: most leaders think AI is making it harder to assess
candidates, yet a meaningful share still allow AI use during interviews.

## Key Findings

- 71% of surveyed engineering leaders say AI is making technical skills
  harder to assess.
- 62% of organizations still prohibit AI use during technical interviews.
- AI-use policy varies sharply by region: 38% of US organizations allow AI
  use in interviews vs. 68% in China.
- Assessment format also differs by region: live technical interviews are
  used by 79% of US orgs vs. 87% in China; take-home projects are more
  common in the US (45%) than China (20%).
- Leaders estimate over half of candidates use AI during interviews even
  when instructed not to (survey gives this as a leader estimate, not a
  measured behavioral statistic — worth being precise about that
  distinction if this becomes a post).
