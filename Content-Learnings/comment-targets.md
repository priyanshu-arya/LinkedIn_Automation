`hook-formulas.md`, `humanizer-rules.md`, `algorithm-rules.md`, and
`story-bank.md` (all system-populated or system-appended living docs), this

whose posts are consistently worth commenting on. `/plan-week` (Phase 21,
Content Planner, REQUIREMENTS.md §32) reads this list when assigning 2-3
comment targets to each filled day in the week's plan.
