Content Playbook for Substack (both Articles and Notes) — the system's
accumulated, evidence-backed rules for this platform specifically. Same
evidence bar as `playbook.md`: every rule cites the post(s) it was derived
from, and nothing is written here on fewer than 3 published posts' worth of
evidence.

This starts empty: nothing has been published yet, and Substack has no
Buffer-style automated metrics pull — analytics here depend on the user
manually entering numbers from their Substack dashboard (see the Substack
analytics gap noted in the pipeline's known-gaps list). `update-playbook
substack` will populate this once that data exists. Track Articles and
Notes patterns separately within this file (they're different formats with
different audiences-in-the-moment) rather than averaging them together.
